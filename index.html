Projects

P2 Gold: Spiritbound

Contributions: I independently developed this entire project. This included designing and implementing the core "Spirit World" swap mechanic, which shifts the active state of platforms, hazards, and moving obstacles in real time, which is the central puzzle-platforming mechanic. I built the player controller, including movement and jumping, and designed and scripted multiple hazard types, including instant-death spikes and water, a rising water sequence, crushing obstacles, and moving platforms. I also implemented checkpoints and the respawn system, designed all levels with escalating difficulty, created the title screen, and added background music, sound effect transitions, and visual effects/sprites.

Art and music are not my own original work! Credits
